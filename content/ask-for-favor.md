# Asking for a favour

## Goal
They should be able to say yes in one word, or no without guilt, and either way feel respected.

## Structure
1. **Context in one line** (why you're asking them specifically, if there is a reason).
2. **The ask, specific**: what, when, how long, what it involves.
3. **Name the cost honestly**: "it's about an hour", "you'd need a car".
4. **A real out.**
5. **What you'll do in return, if natural.** Do not manufacture reciprocity.

## Principles
- Vague asks ("could you help me with something?") force them to say yes before they know what it is. That feels like a trap.
- Pre-apologising ("sorry to ask") frames the request as an imposition. A clear ask with an out is not an imposition.
- People like being asked for things they are good at. If that is why you are asking them, say so.

## Examples

**Ride to the airport, text:**
> Any chance you could drop me at the airport Thursday around 6am? I know it's early. If it's a pain, I'll grab a cab, no problem at all. Coffee's on me either way.

**Ask someone to read your work:**
> You're the best editor I know, so I'm going to be cheeky: would you have 20 minutes this week to read a two-page draft? Honest notes only. If you're underwater, tell me and I'll ask someone else.

**Ask to borrow something:**
> Could I borrow your projector for Saturday night? I'd pick it up Friday and bring it back Sunday. If you need it that weekend, no stress.

**Bigger ask, email:**
> Hi Priya,
> I'm applying for the residency in March and they want a reference from someone who's seen my work up close. You'd be my first choice, but I know it's a real chunk of time, probably an hour to write. The deadline is the 20th. If that's not doable, I completely understand and I'd appreciate a heads-up so I can ask elsewhere.
> Thanks either way,
> Leo

## Avoid
- "I hate to ask but"
- Hiding the size of the ask.
- Following up within 24 hours unless it's time-sensitive.
- "You owe me one" energy.

## Follow-up if no reply
After 3 to 5 days: "No worries if the airport thing doesn't work, just let me know by Tuesday so I can sort a cab."
