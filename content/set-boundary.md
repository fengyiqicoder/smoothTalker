# Setting a boundary

## Goal
The limit is clear, it is about your behaviour (what you will do), and it survives the first round of pushback.

## Structure
1. **Warm opener** that affirms the relationship.
2. **The boundary as something you will do**, not something they must do. "I'm going to stop answering calls after 9pm" holds up better than "you need to stop calling late."
3. **One short reason, optional.**
4. **What you're offering instead** (a time that works, an alternative).
5. **Stop.** No paragraph of justification. Justification is an invitation to negotiate.

## Principles
- A boundary is a statement of what you will do. It is not a request for permission.
- The first time you set it, expect pushback. The pushback is not a sign you were wrong.
- Repeat the same sentence rather than adding new reasons. New reasons are new targets.
- You can be kind and immovable at the same time. Warm tone, firm content.
- Do not set a boundary you will not keep. Every broken boundary makes the next one weaker.

## Examples

**Parent who calls constantly:**
> Mum, I love talking to you and I want to keep it that way. I can't do daily calls with work the way it is. I'm going to call you Sundays, properly, for as long as you like. During the week I'll text.

**Friend who only calls to vent:**
> I care about you and I've noticed our calls lately are mostly you going through a hard time, which I get. I don't have the capacity to be that person every time right now. I'm still here, and I'd love for some of our time to just be fun.

**Relative who comments on your body / life choices:**
> I know you mean well. I'm not going to discuss my weight, so if it comes up I'll change the subject. Everything else, I'm all yours.

**Partner, needing space:**
> I need some evenings that are just mine, not because anything's wrong with us but because I recharge alone. Can we say Tuesdays and Thursdays are mine, and I'll be fully present the rest?

**Someone who keeps asking for money:**
> I'm not able to lend money anymore, to anyone. That's a firm one for me. I'm happy to help in other ways, like looking at a budget with you.

**Group / family chat, muting:**
> I'm going to mute this chat during work hours. If something's urgent, call me.

## When they push back
Say the same thing again, shorter.
> I understand you're not happy about it. Sundays is what I can do.
Third time:
> I've said what I can do. I love you. Talk Sunday.
Then stop replying on that thread.

## Avoid
- "I hope that's okay?"
- "I'm sorry but..."
- "You always..." (turns a boundary into an accusation)
- A list of reasons.
- Setting the boundary in anger. Set it calm, hold it calm.
- Threatening consequences you will not enforce.
