# Declining a request

## Goal
A clean no that does not require a lie, a lecture, or a negotiation.

## Structure
1. **Acknowledge the ask** so they know you understood it (not that you agree).
2. **The no**, plainly. "I can't" or "I'm not able to" or "I'm going to say no to this one."
3. **Optional single reason.** Only if it is true and not arguable ("I'm at capacity", "I don't lend money to friends, it's a rule I keep for everyone").
4. **Offer a smaller yes if you genuinely have one** (a referral, a shorter version, a later date). If you do not, skip this step. A fake alternative is worse than none.
5. **Warm neutral close.**

## Principles
- A no with a reason invites debate on the reason. A no with a rule ("I don't do X") is much harder to argue with.
- "Right now" makes the no temporary. Only use it if it is.
- Do not apologise for having limits. One "sorry" maximum, and only if you genuinely regret it.

## Examples

**Friend asks to borrow money:**
> I'm going to say no on this one. I have a rule about not lending to friends, because I've watched it go badly for people I love. If it would help, I'm happy to sit down with you and look at options.

**Colleague asks you to take on their work:**
> I can see you're slammed. I can't take this on, I'm already past capacity through the end of the month. If it helps, I can walk you through how I set up the template so it goes faster.

**Family asks you to host again:**
> I love having everyone over, and I can't do it this year. Could we rotate and do it at yours, or split a place?

**Someone asks for free professional advice/work:**
> I'd rather be upfront than give you a half answer: this is the kind of thing I do professionally, so I can't do it for free. If you want to book a proper session, here's the link. Otherwise, [resource] is a really good starting point.

**Stranger or acquaintance, low stakes:**
> Not something I can help with, but good luck with it.

## Avoid
- "I'll see what I can do" when you already know.
- Explaining your finances, your calendar, your mental health as justification. You do not owe it.
- Ghosting the request. A no delivered quickly is a kindness; silence is not.

## If they guilt-trip
> I understand you're disappointed. My answer's the same.
Then stop replying on that thread. Do not re-explain.
