# Money between friends

## Goal
The money gets sorted and nobody feels judged. Treat it as admin. The moment it becomes about character, it costs more than the money.

## Structure
1. **Casual opener.**
2. **The number and what it was for**, factual.
3. **The easiest way to pay**, stated.
4. **Optional deadline framed around your needs, not their failing.**
5. **Light close.**

## Principles
- Be specific and fast. The longer a debt sits, the more awkward it gets for both of you.
- Send the number, not a hint. "Whenever you get a chance" is a hint; "£42 for the tickets, here's my link" is a request.
- No shame, no story. They almost certainly forgot.
- If it is a pattern with someone, stop fronting money for them. That is the fix, not a better reminder.
- If you decide to let it go, actually let it go. Do not let it go and then resent it.

## Examples

**First reminder, casual:**
> Hey, whenever you're near your phone: £42 for the gig tickets, my link's [link]. No rush, just so I don't forget.

**Second reminder:**
> Bumping the £42 for the tickets, I've got rent going out Friday so it'd be a help before then.

**Larger amount, direct:**
> Can we sort the £300 from the trip? I've been carrying it a while and it's starting to matter on my end. If it's easier in two chunks that's fine, just let me know when.

**Splitting a bill when someone's underpaying:**
> Let's just split it evenly, it's easier than working out who had what. £28 each.

**Proposing a split before a trip (prevents most problems):**
> Let's do a shared pot for the trip, everyone puts in £200 up front and we settle at the end. Saves the awkward maths.

**Someone asks you to cover them again:**
> I can't front it this time, I'm keeping things tight this month. Can you book your own and we'll meet there?

**Deciding to write it off:**
> Don't worry about the £15, call it even for the lift last week.

## Avoid
- "I hate to bring this up, but..."
- "I'm not made of money, you know."
- Bringing it up in front of others.
- Sarcasm.
- Vague hints ("remember that thing I paid for?").
- Letting it sit for months and then dumping it all at once.
- Sending a payment request app notification with a passive-aggressive memo.
