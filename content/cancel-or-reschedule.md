# Cancelling or rescheduling

## Goal
They know immediately, they know it is not about them, and they have a new time before they finish reading.

## Structure
1. **Lead with the change** in the first sentence.
2. **One reason, short and true.** No reason at all for very casual plans.
3. **Acknowledge the cost to them**, proportional to the notice.
4. **Two concrete alternatives**, not "sometime".
5. **For last-minute cancels: offer to cover a cost or make it up.**

## Principles
- The earlier you tell them, the shorter the apology needs to be.
- Cancelling twice in a row requires you to be the one who reschedules and shows up.
- Do not over-explain. "Work thing came up" is a complete sentence.
- Do not cancel by leaving them hanging. A cancel that comes 3 hours late is two offences.

## Examples

**Advance notice, casual:**
> Need to move Thursday, something's come up. Could do Friday or Sunday afternoon?

**Same day, friend:**
> I'm so sorry to do this on the day: I have to cancel tonight, I've been hit with a migraine. I know you'd planned around it. Can I take you out next week, my treat? Tuesday or Thursday?

**Reschedule with a client / professional:**
> Hi Maria,
> I need to move our 2pm on Wednesday, a conflict I can't shift has come up. I'm sorry for the short notice. I'm free Thursday 10 to 12 or Friday afternoon. Would either work? If neither does, send me a couple of times and I'll make one fit.
> Thanks for your flexibility,
> Ben

**Second cancellation in a row:**
> I'm aware I've cancelled on you twice now and I hate that. This one's on me to fix: I've booked Saturday 1pm at [place]. I'll be there early.

**Cancelling something you don't want to reschedule:**
> I'm going to pull out of Sunday. Nothing dramatic, I just need a quiet weekend. Enjoy it, and let's find another time soon.

**Group plan, you're the one dropping:**
> I've got to drop out of Saturday, sorry all. Don't change anything for me, have a great time.

## Avoid
- "Would it be okay if we rescheduled?" (you are not asking permission; you are informing).
- A long medical/family story.
- "Rain check?" with no date.
- Cancelling with a vague "not feeling it" to someone who made an effort.
- Cancelling via a third party.
