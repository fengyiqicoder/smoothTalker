# Turning someone down romantically

## Goal
No ambiguity, no cruelty, no false hope, no lecture. They can walk away with dignity.

## Structure
1. **Appreciation, specific and brief.**
2. **The no, clear.** "I'm not feeling a romantic connection." Not "I'm not sure" or "maybe later".
3. **No reason, or a reason that is about you/fit, not about their flaws.**
4. **Warm close.** Do not offer friendship unless you mean it.

## Principles
- Ambiguity is the cruel option. "Let's see" makes them wait. A clean no is respect.
- Do not list their faults. They did not ask for feedback.
- Do not over-apologise. You are not doing anything wrong.
- Scale the channel to the relationship: a message is fine for one or two dates; a call for a few weeks; in person for months.
- Ghosting after a real date is the one thing to never do.
- Do not respond to arguments about your decision. It is not a debate.

## Examples

**After one date:**
> Thanks for last night, I had a nice time. I'm not feeling a romantic connection, so I don't think we should do a second date. Wishing you well.

**After a few dates, message:**
> I've really enjoyed getting to know you and I've been thinking about it: I don't feel this going where it should for me, and I'd rather be honest now than drag it out. You've been lovely. I'm sorry it's not the answer you wanted.

**Someone asks you out and you're not interested:**
> That's really kind of you to say, and I'm flattered. I don't see you that way, though, so I'll say no. Hope that doesn't make things weird.

**Friend confesses feelings:**
> Thank you for telling me, I know that wasn't easy. I don't feel the same way. I really value you as a friend and I hope we can keep that, but I understand if you need some space first.

**Ending a short relationship (weeks to a few months), ideally a call, script:**
> I want to be straight with you because you deserve that. I've realised I'm not in this the way I'd want to be, and it's not fair to either of us for me to keep going. This isn't about anything you did. I'm sorry.

**Someone from an app before meeting:**
> I've enjoyed chatting but I don't think we're a match. Good luck out there.

**If they push for a reason:**
> I don't think a list of reasons would help either of us. It's about fit, not about you being wrong.

## Avoid
- "It's not you, it's me" verbatim (cliche, reads as a lie even when true).
- "Maybe in the future."
- "You'll find someone great."
- "I'm not ready for a relationship" if you are (they will find out).
- Explaining at length.
- Offering friendship as a consolation prize when you do not want it.
- Replying to a hostile response. One line: "I understand you're upset. I'm going to leave it here." Then done.
